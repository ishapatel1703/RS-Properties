Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to buy a property?+

Most of our transactions close within 30–45 days from shortlisting to registration. With a pre-approved home loan and clear title, it can be even faster. We guide you through each step so there are no delays.

What is your brokerage fee?+

Our consultation and shortlisting is completely free. Brokerage is charged only on successful transaction completion — typically 1–2% depending on property type and value. This will be clearly agreed upfront with no hidden charges.

Do you help with home loan processing?+

Yes! We're tied up with 10+ leading banks including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and PNB Housing. We compare rates, help with documentation, and ensure the fastest possible approval — at no extra charge to you.

Are all listed properties RERA-registered?+

For new projects and plots, we strictly list only RERA-approved properties. For resale properties, we conduct full title and encumbrance checks. We will never recommend a property with clouded title or legal disputes.

Do you serve NRI clients?+

Absolutely. We have a dedicated NRI advisory team. We provide virtual property tours, handle power of attorney, manage all paperwork remotely, and ensure RBI/FEMA compliance for NRI property purchases in India.
